HOW TO PLAY

You are Sugeng Wibowo, getaway driver of Wilwatikta. Run 12 story missions for Agus the fixer, stay ahead of Komisaris Wanto and the WPD, and build your name from street nobody to legend of the sunset city.

SURVIVAL

Stars = wanted level. Break line of sight to cool down. At 3 stars the WPD helicopter joins with a spotlight; at 3+ stars expect roadblocks. Busted or wasted costs you cash and restarts the mission. Spend cash at Bengkel Bambang (G) — engine, armor, tires, paint.
